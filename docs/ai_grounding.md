# AI Grounding & Deterministic Integrity

To protect students from math hallucinations (e.g. incorrect attendance percentage calculations), TrackX overrides AI-generated numbers.

## Hard Grounding Rules
- All percentages, days until exam, and credits remaining are calculated locally on-device.
- AI responses display source links pointing directly to the reference records.
- Tapping a reference opens the corresponding Subject detail, Exam workspace, or Planner item.
